## 2. Match Format & In-Game Rules

* **Target Duration / Games:** Play exactly 17 games per match.


* **Warm-up:** Strictly 5 minutes maximum to preserve court time for play.


* **Silver Point at Deuce:** At 40–40, play one standard advantage point. If the score returns to Deuce, play a Golden Point (Sudden Death). The receiving team selects the receiving side/player.


* **Side changes:** In order to keep up the pace of play (but still not overly advantage one team), please change sides every 3 games.



---

## 3. League Points System

| Component | Points Awarded | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Games Won** | 1 Point per game | Every single game counts towards your league points tally (e.g. 10–7 awards 10 game pts to the winner, 7 game pts to the loser). |
| **Match Win Bonus** | +2 Points | Awarded to the overall match winner. |
| **Draw Bonus** | +1 Point each | Awarded to both teams if the match ends tied due to time expiration. |

---

## 4. Time Expiration (1-Hour Slot Limitations)

When playing within a 60-minute booking:

* **Hard Stop:** When court booking time expires, play ends immediately.


* **Unfinished Games:** Any game underway when time expires does not count towards the score unless the deciding point was completed before time was called.


* **Result Stands:** The score at time expiration is the official match result (e.g. 10–7, 11–6, etc.).


* **Declaring a Winner:** The leading team wins the match, keeping their game points plus the +2 match win bonus points.



---

## 5. What Happens in the Event of a Draw?

* **Timed-Out Draws:** If the 1-hour limit expires and the score is level (e.g., 7–7 or 8–8), the match will be recorded as a Draw. Both pairs keep their games won and each receive +1 draw bonus point (e.g., an 8–8 score awards 9 points to each pair). Enter the result in the Google form (it doesn't matter who goes down as the winning/losing team in this case).



---

## 6. What happens if we don't reach 17 total games played in the allotted time?

If time runs out before 17 games are completed, then please share the remaining games in order to bring the score up to a full match.

* **Example #1:** a match stands 10–3 and the hour runs out. 13 games have been played. Divide the remaining games (e.g. $17 - 13 = 4$) evenly between the 2 teams to bring the score to 12–5, which is then recorded as the final score.


* **Example #2:** a match stands 8–6 and the hour runs out. 14 games have been played. 3 games are remaining, of which 2 are awarded to the winning team and 1 to the losing team. The final score is then recorded as 10–7. Basically, if there's an odd number of games remaining, divide the remaining games evenly between the 2 teams and then award 1 extra to the winning team.

## 7. Cross-divisional Games

One of the main pain points in organising this box league is the uncertainty surrounding player's current levels. In order to gain more information (and in preparation for the next iteration of this box league), I would encourage pairings to organise games also between teams from the other division, and record these scores using the Google form. You won't earn league points from these games, but they will hopefully encourage competition and provide valuable information for a bigger & better next iteration of the league.
